How to buy $AUZ

Three legal ways to get USDC or SOL on Solana in Uzbekistan: cash via Paynet, licensed exchange via Coinpay, or fiat gateway via CityPay.

Cash

Method 1 — Buy with cash at a Paynet kiosk

  1. Go to any Paynet kiosk near you (over 16,000 terminals).
  2. Select the section «CityPay — purchase of crypto assets at info kiosks».
  3. Scan the QR code using the Paynet mobile app.
  4. Top up your Paynet card with 200,000 UZS (or from 30,000 UZS).
  5. In the Paynet app, convert your UZS balance to SOL (Solana).
  6. Send the SOL to your Jupiter wallet.
  7. Open Jupiter Swap and exchange your SOL for AUZ.
💡 Commission: 2% (included in the amount). Rate is fixed for 30 seconds at the kiosk.
⚡ Open Jupiter Swap (SOL → AUZ)
Licensed

Method 2 — Buy via Coinpay (licensed Binance gateway)

  1. Register at coinpay.uz — the official licensed gateway to Binance in Uzbekistan.
  2. Top up your balance in UZS via Humo/Uzcard bank card.
  3. Buy USDT or SOL on the exchange inside Coinpay.
  4. Withdraw SOL to your Jupiter wallet (Phantom or Solflare).
  5. Open Jupiter Swap and exchange SOL for AUZ.
→ Go to Coinpay.uz ⚡ Jupiter Swap
Licensed

Method 3 — Buy via CityPay (fiat gateway)

  1. Register at citypay.io — licensed crypto shop with UZS support.
  2. Top up via Humo card, Uzcard, or Paynet.
  3. Buy SOL (Solana) directly in the CityPay wallet.
  4. Send SOL to your Jupiter wallet.
  5. Swap SOL for AUZ on Jupiter.
→ Go to CityPay.io ⚡ Jupiter Swap
